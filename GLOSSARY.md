# Career Publication

Language for turning private career evidence into explicitly approved public
representations.

## Language

**Career record**:
Private evidence about a person's experience, contributions, skills, interests,
and outcomes, including sources and unresolved questions.
_Avoid_: Public profile, published CV

**Publication draft**:
Proposed public wording or content changes that are awaiting approval for their
specified destinations.
_Avoid_: Approved content, published content

**Approved content**:
Exact content explicitly authorised by the person for a specified public
destination; that approval does not extend to revised wording or other destinations.
_Avoid_: Approved facts, blanket approval
